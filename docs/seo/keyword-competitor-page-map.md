# Artisan Lab Network Keyword Universe, Competitors, and Page Map

Finalized: August 22, 2026  
Scope: United States, B2B optical laboratory discovery, local lab discovery, provider resources, product education, ownership/partnership, and account-opening intent.

## Strategy

The search program should connect three layers:

1. **Network demand:** independent optical lab, wholesale optical lab, optical laboratory partner, and doctor-owned/independent alternatives.
2. **Lab demand:** the Pike, Peak, and Pacific brand names plus Indianapolis/Midwest, Colorado/Mountain West, and Portland/Pacific Northwest service intent.
3. **Solution demand:** digital/freeform lenses, anti-reflective treatments, specialty/high-index lenses, practice resources, optical engineering, lab switching, and account opening.

One primary page owns each topic cluster. Lab pages may mention shared capabilities, but they should not all compete for the exact same national head term. The homepage owns the network entity; `/switch-to-artisan` owns comparison/switching intent; product hubs own product terms; individual lab pages own brand-plus-region terms.

No third-party keyword-volume database was available for this deliverable. Priority is therefore based on observed Search Console demand, commercial intent, current rankings, site capabilities, and competitor language. Validate volume and difficulty in an approved SEO platform before using numeric forecasts.

### Monthly competitor review

For the five core search competitors—Walman, IDOC Labs, Cherry, Robertson, and Laramy-K—track:

- Ranking pages for `independent optical lab`, `wholesale optical lab`, and high-intent variants.
- New pages and articles, title/H1 changes, FAQ and schema use.
- Account-opening, pricing, comparison, and contact CTAs.
- Product/capability coverage, evidence, case studies, and practitioner education.
- Referring domains earned by tools, research, education, or industry partnerships.

Use Singer and Frecker as Pike local comparators, and Orange Optical as a Pacific regional comparator. Expand the Colorado set only after a SERP review identifies a recurring local wholesale competitor; consumer optical stores should not be treated as direct B2B competitors.

## Cannibalization Rules

1. The homepage owns `independent optical lab network`; `/switch-to-artisan` owns comparison and switching language.
2. Each lab page owns its brand plus its service geography. Do not create separate thin city pages unless there is a real local presence and unique content.
3. `/artisan-ar` is the category hub; each treatment page owns only its product name and verified differentiator.
4. A future Tokai hub owns `Tokai lenses`; news releases should target announcement-specific queries and link to the hub.
5. Practice Matters has one indexable archive and self-canonical article URLs. `/newsletter`, `/newsletters`, and the Practice Matters hub should not all compete for the same newsletter term; choose one primary hub and redirect or canonicalize the others deliberately.
6. Filter/search URLs in Provider Resources should not create indexable parameter combinations.

## Measurement Tie-In

Every mapped commercial page should be evaluated with:

- Search Console query, landing page, impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position.
- GA4 organic sessions and engaged sessions.
- `generate_lead`, `open_account`, `partner_inquiry`, `schedule_meeting`, and `click_phone`.
- `lab_name` and `site_version` on all relevant actions.
- Organic qualified actions per 100 sessions, not traffic alone.

This connects keyword growth to the business question: which search themes, pages, and labs produce meaningful activity.
